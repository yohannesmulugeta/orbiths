- Training and installation photographs are genuine original OrbitHS archive images, exported from 1280px originals. The gallery retains its real photographs. Original partner logos and team portraits are preserved.
- The mobile-clinic image is labeled as an illustrative design concept in the homepage caption and solution catalog. It does not represent an actual installation or a specific product on sale.

## Generated concept

Created using the built-in image generation tool and saved to `public/images/mobile-clinic-hq.webp`, with a 640px and 1280px version.

Prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: healthcare company website mobile-clinic concept illustration. Create a crisp high-resolution landscape 3:2 architectural product visualization of one plausible modern mobile medical clinic vehicle, white medium-duty truck cab with a clean rectangular clinic box body, restrained bright blue stripe matching #0397D6, no company logos, no writing, no red cross. Three-quarter front-side view, vehicle entirely visible with all wheels and roof intact, parked outside an understated contemporary healthcare building. Natural bright daylight, elegant neutral concrete surroundings, blue sky, subtle landscaping. Professional realistic concept render, sharp body edges and convincing materials, no people, no dramatic effects, no advertising text or watermarks. It must communicate a generic design concept, not a branded real installation.

## Limits

Historical company photos and some original logos/portraits still depend on their source quality. Enlarging these cannot recover detail; approved original camera files would improve them further.
