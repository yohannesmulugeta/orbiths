# OrbitHS design review and refinement

Reviewed the live GitHub Pages site on 9 October 2026.

## What weakened the previous design
- Dark navy and lime dominated the first screen, despite the official logo's bright blue identity.
- Oversized text covered the hero photograph and solution cards.
- Partners appeared late on the homepage as a small six-logo grid.
- Large headings, wide gaps and repeated aspirational statements made the homepage unnecessarily long.
- The sticky header was constrained by the root container's overflow behavior.

## Implemented
1. Separate blue hero copy from the unobscured medical photograph, with a short explanation of the company's actual offer.
2. Use logo blue #0397D6 prominently through accents, blue surfaces and primary interactions. Use darker blue #0276AA behind small white text for contrast. Keep lime #C1D72E as a restrained accent.
3. Place all 30 original partner logos directly below the hero in a moving strip. Preserve logo colors and proportions. Provide play/pause, hover/focus pause, manual horizontal scrolling and reduced-motion support. Duplicated animation copies are hidden from assistive technology.
4. Put solution names, photo captions and category numbers outside photographs.
5. Shorten repeated sections, reduce heading sizes and spacing, and feature original training/installation photographs lower on the homepage.
6. Add partner directory search with result counts and empty-state guidance.
7. Use blue on the service approach, contact panels, call to action and footer. Keep white content areas for readability.
8. Restore sticky-header behavior without creating a root scroll container.

## What would improve business credibility next
1. Three approved project stories: facility/client name, the need, equipment supplied, installation/training scope, real photographs and a verified result.
2. Approved product brochures and higher-resolution original images for the smaller product references. Do not fabricate stock, specifications, certifications or performance figures.
3. A confirmed company mailbox and real enquiry delivery, with spam protection and honest delivery confirmation. The current enquiry action remains a local download.
4. Confirm current team roles, partner relationships and contact details, and obtain publication approval for client references.
5. Improve search visibility before moving to orbiths.com: route-specific metadata, crawlable URLs, original-page redirects, sitemap and agreed analytics.

The visual changes improve presentation; verified project proof and working enquiry delivery are the next practical priorities.
